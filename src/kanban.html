Drag a card or its handle to another column. On a phone, drag the top-right handle; swipe elsewhere to scroll.

Cards with is-disabled or aria-disabled="true" stay visible but cannot be dragged.

Override an item with data-kanban-color and data-kanban-text-color. Set data-kanban-accent and optionally data-kanban-accent-position (top, right, bottom, or left) for an edge color. The older kanban-card-accent classes and CSS variables remain supported. Use kanban.setZoom(value), kanban.getZoom(), and kanban.resetZoom() for board scaling.

Touch drag handles

Movable cards in this demo have a discreet top-right handle with a 44px touch target and the <i class="material-icons">drag_indicator</i> handle. Drag it to reorder cards or move between columns on a phone. Focus a handle and use up/down arrows to reorder, or left/right arrows to change columns. Disabled cards and boards with draggable: false have no handle.

The pointer helper lives in src/components/card-drag-handles.ts with its imported SCSS. It supplements the npm component’s native desktop dragging. See its integration in src/main.ts for the move callback and count updates.
