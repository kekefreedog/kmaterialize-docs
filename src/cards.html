Cards are a convenient means of displaying content composed of different types of objects. They’re also well-suited for presenting similar objects whose size or supported actions can vary considerably, like photos with captions of variable length.

Background Image Card

Add card-image-alt to card to cover the whole card with a background image. A dark overlay keeps the light text and text buttons visible. An optional card-action footer sits at the bottom on a theme-colored surface.

The overlay is automatic. Existing markup with <div class="overlay"></div> directly inside the card also works. Background images are decorative; include meaningful information in the card text.

Cards start at a minimum height of 186px and grow with their content. Set --card-image-alt-height to change that minimum, --card-image-position to adjust the crop, or --card-image-alt-overlay to replace the overlay color or gradient. Use small, medium, or large for a fixed-height card with scrollable content.

<div class="card card-image-alt" style="background-image: url('images/sample-1.jpg'); --card-image-alt-height: 240px; --card-image-position: center top; --card-image-alt-overlay: linear-gradient(transparent, var(--md-sys-color-scrim));">
  ...
</div>

Add gradient to the card itself: card card-image-alt gradient. The image fades from clear at the top to dark at the bottom, with the content aligned over the darker area. This also supports an explicit overlay element and the --card-image-alt-overlay customization.

Card Badges

Place a card-badges group inside card-image to display custom labels above the image. Badges sit in the top-right corner by default; add left to move the group to the top-left. Multiple badges wrap when space is limited.

<div class="card">
  <div class="card-image">
    <img src="images/sample-1.jpg" alt="Mountain lake surrounded by trees">
    <span class="card-title gradient">Lakeside escape</span>
    <div class="card-badges">
      <span class="badge">Featured</span>
      <span class="badge" style="--card-badge-background: var(--md-sys-color-tertiary-container); --card-badge-color: var(--md-sys-color-on-tertiary-container);">New</span>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="card-content"><p>Discover your next destination.</p></div>
</div>

Set --card-badge-background and --card-badge-color on a badge, group, or card to customize its colors. Use paired theme tokens so they adapt to light and dark mode. The badges also work with small, medium, and large image cards.

Labels allow clicks through to the image. For an interactive badge, use <a class="badge" href="…">Featured</a> with a descriptive label. No JavaScript is required.

Tabs in Cards

You can add tabs to your cards by putting a .tabs element inbetween your header content and your tab content, directly inside the card - there's no separate wrapper div for it in v2. 

Copied!content_copy
<div class="card">
  <div class="card-content">
    <p>I am a very simple card. I am good at containing small bits of information. I am convenient because I require little markup to use effectively.</p>
  </div>
  <ul class="tabs tabs-fixed-width">
    <li class="tab"><a href="#test4">Test 1</a></li>
    <li class="tab"><a class="active" href="#test5">Test 2</a></li>
    <li class="tab"><a href="#test6">Test 3</a></li>
  </ul>
  <div class="card-content">
    <div id="test4">Test 1</div>
    <div id="test5">Test 2</div>
    <div id="test6">Test 3</div>
  </div>
</div>

Card Sizes

Add small, medium, or large to card for heights of 300px, 400px, and 500px. The grid controls the width.

Like the image-sizing example, each image fills a consistent 180px area using object-fit: cover. Landscape, portrait, and square images crop without stretching. The content uses the remaining space and scrolls when needed; actions stay visible at the bottom.

Small · 300px

A compact card for a short introduction. This landscape image uses the same crop height as the other examples. Scroll this content area to read the full description while the action remains visible.

Explore
Medium · 400px
More room

A portrait source does not make this image taller than its neighbors. The fixed crop leaves more space for details in a medium card. Longer descriptions remain available by scrolling this region.

Explore
Large · 500px
Space for detail

The large card keeps the same image height and dedicates the extra space to its content. Use it for a richer description, product details, or supporting information. The action stays below the text, with no overlap.

Explore
<div class="card medium">
  <div class="card-image">
    <img src="photo.jpg" alt="A description of the photograph">
  </div>
  <div class="card-content" tabindex="0" role="region" aria-label="Card details">
    <span class="card-title">Card title</span>
    <p>Content can scroll when it exceeds the available space.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card-action"><a href="details.html">Read more</a></div>
</div>

The classes also work without images or actions. For horizontal cards, combine horizontal with a size class and use the usual card-stacked wrapper; the image fills the card height and 40% of its width.

To adjust a vertical card’s image height or crop position, set --card-image-height or --card-image-position. Cards without a size class retain their natural image and content sizing. No JavaScript is needed.

<div class="card large" style="--card-image-height: 220px; --card-image-position: center top;">
  …
</div>
